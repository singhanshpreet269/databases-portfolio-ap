# Reflections

[Back to main portfolio](../README.md)

This folder holds one reflection per class session, named `ref01.md` to `refnn.md`. I write each one on the day of the session (under 10 minutes), while the interesting points are still fresh.

## What each reflection contains

- **Take-away:** what I want to remember and what my advice would be (outstanding points, not just a summary).
- **What was good?**
- **What was difficult?** Where I struggled and the root cause.
- **Exploration and show stoppers:** any good or fun "struggling", and anything where I should reach out to peers or instructors.
- **Interaction with peers and instructors.**
- **How I verified AI suggestions:** what I asked, how I checked it, and where it was wrong.
- **Help and extra work:** who helped me, who I helped, and anything I presented, implemented or fixed for the class.

## Recurring themes

- **Measure first, then decide:** numbers (row counts, file size, query plans) beat assumptions.
- **Verify before you trust:** this applies to AI-written SQL as much as to an old schema that "just works".
- **Explaining helps learning:** helping pod mates with the tools and the ER diagram made me understand the material better myself.
